# MM showroom design language

The public website takes its cues from a performance showroom and a vehicle specification sheet. The identity comes from the dealer’s place, specialty and personal service: Wingfield, HSV and Holden, Madi, and the supplied customer reviews.

## Typography and colour

Barlow Condensed provides the large, direct headings and vehicle names. Manrope carries the body copy and controls. System monospace is limited to stock references, section labels and small technical details. Prices and kilometres use tabular figures.

The canvas is near-black (`#0c0d0e`), with warm white (`#f1eee8`) text. Bright red (`#f03b4c`) marks active states and text links. Action buttons use a deeper red (`#d82537`) so their white text stays readable. A warm paper section (`#ece8df`) introduces Madi and the Wingfield showroom. Red is a signal, rather than the background of every component.

## Layout and details

Use thin rules, square corners, open spacing and real stock references. Keep vehicle photographs large and put year, model, variant, odometer, gearbox, fuel and price in a predictable order. The stock page stays wide; the vehicle page retains its full-width gallery and right-hand summary. Related cars collapse to a readable single column on phones.

The home page moves from the showroom introduction to available stock, the people behind the business, customer reviews and a direct phone invitation. Numbered section labels provide a repeated visual detail. The owner quotes come from the supplied business profile, without invented review badges or ratings.

## Content and interaction

Write as a local car business: name the model, make the next action clear and tell customers who they will speak to. Use the supplied address and phone. Link to Google Maps instead of drawing a fictional map.

Existing photography and inventory remain preview content. The hero uses an existing wheel-and-brake close-up so its visual supports performance without implying a specific HSV model. Do not invent vehicle history, inspections, customer outcomes or dealer credentials.

Stock filters, saved cars, gallery navigation, enquiries and the separate admin concepts retain their existing behavior. Use restrained image and link hover states, visible keyboard focus and the reduced-motion rules. Keep the existing wordmark text and original logo asset intact.
