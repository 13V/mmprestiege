# Your website: owner's guide

**MM Prestige Motors** · mmprestigemotors.vercel.app

Your website shows the cars you have for sale and the ones you've sold, takes enquiries and finance applications, and links to your Instagram, TikTok and Facebook. You keep the cars up to date yourself, from your phone, with the stock manager.

## The stock manager

### Signing in

1. On your phone, open **mmprestigemotors.vercel.app/stock/manage** (or your own web address followed by /stock/manage, once it's connected).
2. Enter your password. You stay signed in on that phone for 30 days.
3. Add it to your home screen so it opens like an app. On an iPhone, in Safari: **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. On Android, in Chrome: the **⋮** menu, then **Add to Home screen**.

Keep the password to yourself. If you think someone else knows it, ask your web developer to change it; that signs out every phone.

### Adding a car

1. Tap **Add a car**.
2. **Photos.** Tap **Add photos** and take them or choose them from your library. The first photo is the cover on the website; use the arrows under each photo to change the order. Up to 40 photos.
3. **The car.** The year, make and model (these three are needed), then the variant or badge (for example "Gen-F2 Clubsport R8"), body, colour, gearbox, engine, fuel, power and kilometres.
4. **Price and listing.** The price, an optional tag shown on the photo (JUST ARRIVED, LOW KMS, PRICE REDUCED and so on) and a description: what makes the car special, such as its history, service records, options and condition.
5. **Registration.** The rego, state, rego expiry and VIN, for your own records. None of this is ever shown on the website. The stock number fills itself in (MM101, MM102 and so on).
6. Tap **Put on the website**. It's live in about a minute. Not ready yet? Tap **Save draft** and finish it later; drafts never show on the website.

A car can't go on the website without the year, make, model, kilometres, price and at least one photo. If something's missing, the stock manager tells you what.

### Changing a car

Your stock is in three tabs: **On the website**, **Drafts** and **Sold**. Tap a car to open it.

- **Save changes** after changing anything: the price, photos, description and so on.
- **Mark as sold** moves it to the sold cars on the website, with a SOLD tag.
- **Back on sale** puts a sold car back up, if a sale falls through.
- **Delete this car** removes it and its photos completely. This can't be undone.

### The sample listings

Until you put your first car on the website, it shows six sample listings, marked as samples with example prices. When your first real car goes on, they disappear by themselves.

You can reuse them: on the empty Your stock screen, tap **Copy in the current listings**. They come in as drafts with their photos. Check each one's price, kilometres and details before you put it on the website.

### Good photos

- Hold the phone sideways: landscape photos look best.
- Start with a front three-quarter shot for the cover, then the sides, rear, interior, engine bay, odometer and wheels, and any marks a buyer should know about.
- A clean car and an uncluttered background, in daylight or under the showroom lights.
- Big camera photos are fine: they're resized on your phone before they upload, and their location data is removed.

### If something goes wrong

- **"You were signed out."** Sign in again. Your changes are kept.
- **"The stock changed on another device."** Someone saved from another phone or computer. Tap **Reload**, check the car, and save again.
- **A change isn't on the website after a few minutes.** Tap **Update website now** at the bottom of Your stock.
- **A photo says "Didn't upload".** Remove it and add it again. Check your signal.
- Still stuck? Contact your web developer.

## Enquiries

Every form on the website asks for the customer's full name, phone number and email. That covers general enquiries and viewings, finance applications, selling or trading in a car, and asking you to find a car.

Once email is switched on, enquiries arrive at **mmprestigemotors@gmail.com** from "MM Prestige Motors website". The subject says what it is, for example "Finance enquiry: Jane Smith, 2016 HSV GTS". Reply to the email to answer the customer. Turn on Gmail notifications on your phone so you see them straight away.

Until email is switched on, or if sending ever fails, the website lets customers send their enquiry from their own email app, or as a text to 0411 710 409.

### Switching email on

It takes about five minutes, with your web developer:

1. Sign in to the Google account for mmprestigemotors@gmail.com and turn on **2-Step Verification** (Google Account, then Security).
2. Open **myaccount.google.com/apppasswords**, name it "MM Prestige website" and tap **Create**. Google shows a 16-letter password once.
3. Give it to your developer in person or over the phone, not by text or email. They add it to the website and send a test enquiry.

The app password can only send email, and it's separate from your Gmail password, which stays private. To stop the website sending email, delete the app password on that same page.

## Who looks after what

- **You:** the cars, photos and prices in the stock manager, and replying to enquiries.
- **Your web developer:** the website's design and wording, your phone number, email and address on the site, the stock manager's password, email sending, hosting and your web address.
